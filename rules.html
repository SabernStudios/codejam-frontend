Rules

Keep it fair, readable, and student-made. These rules are here so judges can understand your work and compare projects consistently.

  1. The theme will be revealed at the start of CodeJam.
  2. Your project should clearly connect to the theme. The connection can be literal, creative, funny, or abstract.
  3. If judges cannot understand the connection, we may ask you to explain your project before final decisions are made.
  1. You may submit as an individual or as a team of up to four students.
  2. Your GitHub repository must be public or otherwise viewable by judges.
  3. Your description should explain what the project does, how it was built, and how it connects to the theme.
  4. Submissions and edits close at 12:00 AM Eastern on October 28, 2026. Projects received at 11:59 PM on October 27 are still eligible; no late submissions are accepted.
  1. Projects are scored on creativity, functionality, technical effort, presentation, and user experience.
  2. Team projects are judged with team size and project scope in mind.
  3. Your project must be substantially student-made. AI tools may be used for help, but you should understand and be able to explain your code.
  4. Finalists may be asked to walk judges through their code or development process if clarification is needed.
