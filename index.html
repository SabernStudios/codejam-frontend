What is CodeJam?

Make a project around one shared theme.

CodeJam is CSHS's schoolwide coding competition. When the theme is announced, students build a game, app, website, tool, simulation, or other coded project that responds to it. You can work solo or with a team of up to four students.

The goal is simple: make something real, submit it on time, and show the school what you built.
